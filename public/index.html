Where to Find the 12-Digit UTR?

The UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) or UPI Ref ID is a unique 12-digit number provided by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) for every single UPI transaction.

🔵 Google Pay (GPay)

Tap on the transaction → Scroll down to "UPI transaction ID" (starts with numbers, 12 digits, e.g. 429182910291).

🟣 PhonePe

Go to Transaction Details → Look for "UTR" under Debited from account details (12 digits).

🔷 Paytm

In payment details → Locate "UPI Ref No" or "Bank Ref No" (12 digits).
